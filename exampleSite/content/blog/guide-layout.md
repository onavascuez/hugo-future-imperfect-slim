This post uses `layout = "guide"`. The table of contents is built from the
headings of the post. On wide screens it stays next to the text while you
scroll and marks the section you are reading. On narrow screens it sits above
the text.

## Getting started

Add `layout = "guide"` to the front matter of any post. Nothing else is needed.

### Headings

Use second and third level headings to structure the guide. They become the
entries of the table of contents.

### Updated date

If the post has a `lastmod` date different from its `date`, the guide shows
when it was last updated.

## Writing a guide

A guide is a regular post. Everything that works in a post works here.

## Tips

1. Keep headings short: they are also the menu.
2. Prefer a few sections with clear names.
3. Update `lastmod` when you revise the guide.

> A guide is read in pieces. Make every section understandable on its own.

## Summary

Use the guide layout for long, structured content that people come back to.
